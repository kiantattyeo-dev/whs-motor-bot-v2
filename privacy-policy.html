WHS Motor Bot — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 January 2024

1. Introduction

WHS Motor Sdn Bhd operates WHS Motor Bot, an internal social media automation tool. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when using the TikTok API.

2. Who We Are

WHS Motor Bot is used solely by authorised employees of WHS Motor Sdn Bhd to publish promotional content on social media platforms.

3. Information We Collect

Via the TikTok API, we access: basic account info (username, display name) and access tokens to publish content. We do not collect personal data of third parties.

4. How We Use Information

API data is used only to authenticate WHS Motor Bot and publish motorcycle-related promotional content on our own TikTok account. We do not sell or share data.

5. Data Storage and Security

Tokens are stored securely on our internal server, accessible only to authorised personnel.

6. Data Retention

Tokens are retained only as long as needed and deleted when the integration is removed or revoked.

7. Your Rights

You may revoke access at any time via your TikTok account settings.
